Welcome to the world where creativity meets impact. Etsirbella designs is a Kenyan brand that has the interest of the child and women at heart. We are dedicated to transforming the lives of the underprivileged children and upskilling women in the slum through handmade crafts.

The company creates and curates products across hair and beauty, fashion accessories, bags and lifestyle, footwear and gifting, and bespoke and corporate solutions. Its purpose is anchored in a simple model: Buy. Educate. Transform.

Customers purchase quality products that celebrate African beauty and creativity. The enterprise allocates 40% of its revenue toward Etsirbella Scholarship, creating a structured bridge between commercial activity and education-focused social impact. The ambition is to build a strong, scalable African brand in which customer choice, enterprise growth and community transformation reinforce one another.

Behind the label

Designed, cut and stitched for impact.

Etsirbella Designs began in 2018 as a conventional fashion and accessories business. The enterprise grew from a passion for creativity, fashion and customer needs, while the founder's wider work in education and community engagement increasingly revealed the need for sustainable ways to connect business activity with social impact.

In 2023, Etsirbella Designs made a deliberate shift: from being simply a business that sells products to becoming a purpose-driven social impact business. The commercial foundation remained essential, but its role became clearer, build a sustainable enterprise whose success can contribute to opportunities for people and education for children.

Through our Buy. Educate. Transform. model, every purchase helps provide quality education for underprivileged children in Kibera.
