What drives us

Our mission

To make thoughtfully cut, small-batch clothing that treats the wearer and the maker with equal respect — natural fabric, fair pay for our tailors, and pieces built to outlast a single season.

Our vision

To grow into East Africa's most trusted small-batch studio — known not for how much we make, but for how well it's made and how long it lasts.

What we hold to

Our core values

Craftsmanship

Every seam is done by hand, by tailors who take pride in getting the small details right.

Sustainability

Natural fibres and small production runs — nothing made just to sit in a landfill.

Transparency

From fabric sourcing to pricing, we're upfront about where your garment comes from.

Community

We work directly with local tailors and small studios who share our standards.

Authenticity

Our designs reflect real Nairobi style, not a trend borrowed from somewhere else.

Comfort

Clothes should feel as good as they look, so fit and fabric always come before flash.

Timeless design

We design pieces meant to be worn for years, not just for one season.
